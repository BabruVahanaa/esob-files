# ESOB Files — deployment and editorial checklist

Static HTML/CSS/JS website. Deploy the folder to Vercel as a static site (Framework Preset: Other; no build command).

## Before going live
1. Domain URLs are configured for `https://esobfiles.co.uk`. Connect domain DNS to hosting before going live.
2. Add a genuine editorial contact email to `/about/index.html`.
3. Verify event details with Stronghearts / Little Hearts Café and update the event preview if details change.
4. Review all articles for editorial accuracy; these are short explainers, **not tested product reviews** or reported historical news.
5. Confirm and accurately describe the relationship with Stronghearts. Consider `rel="sponsored"` for any links that are advertising or paid placements. Do not use artificial cross-site link networks or fabricated publication dates.
6. Submit `/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after domain verification.

## Editorial dates
All 25 articles are actually published on 10 November 2026. The month shown on each article refers to **topic period** (November 2025–November 2026), not original publication date. Never alter publication dates to simulate history.

## Technical
Accessible semantic HTML, responsive CSS, search/filter without dependencies, article structured data, canonical tags, sitemap, robots file, and internal related-article links. No external tracking scripts.
