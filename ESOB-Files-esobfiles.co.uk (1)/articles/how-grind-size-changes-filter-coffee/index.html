How grind size changes filter coffee

A beginner-friendly guide to extraction and simple adjustments for common filter brewers.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2025-12

Grind size affects how quickly water moves through coffee and how easily flavour compounds dissolve. Finer is not always better.

What to know

If a brew tastes harsh and dries the mouth, try a slightly coarser grind. If it tastes thin or sour, try a finer grind or a longer contact time, changing one variable at a time.

Practical takeaway

Record your dose, water amount and grind setting so improvements are repeatable.
