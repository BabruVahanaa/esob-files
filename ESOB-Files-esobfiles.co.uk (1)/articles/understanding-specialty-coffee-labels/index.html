Understanding specialty coffee labels

A practical explanation of origin, processing, roast level and the information that actually helps buyers choose coffee.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2025-11

A coffee bag can carry a surprising number of claims. Origin names, roast descriptions and tasting notes are useful starting points, but they are not guarantees of quality.

What to know

Look for a clear roast date, useful brewing guidance and transparent sourcing information. If a seller provides farm or cooperative details, consider them alongside freshness and how the coffee tastes in your own cup.

Practical takeaway

For beginners, a small bag from a reputable roaster is often a better experiment than buying several kilograms on the strength of an impressive label.
