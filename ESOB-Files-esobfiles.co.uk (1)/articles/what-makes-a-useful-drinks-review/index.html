What makes a useful drinks review?

Our editorial checklist for comparing flavour, value, ingredients and transparency.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-03

A trustworthy review distinguishes observable product details from personal preference. Price, pack size, ingredients and availability can be checked; taste is subjective.

What to know

A useful tasting note describes sweetness, acidity, texture, aroma and finish without presenting opinion as a laboratory result. Comparisons should use similar serving conditions.

Practical takeaway

ESOB Files does not publish invented hands-on tests. Articles labelled guides are not product reviews.
