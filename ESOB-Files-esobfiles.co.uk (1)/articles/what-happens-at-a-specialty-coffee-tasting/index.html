What happens at a specialty coffee tasting?

A practical preview of tasting formats, flavour vocabulary and questions to ask at an event.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-09

Coffee tastings give guests a chance to compare cups side by side and notice differences in aroma, acidity, sweetness and finish.

What to know

You do not need technical expertise to participate. Describe what you actually taste, ask about brewing methods and compare preferences with others.
