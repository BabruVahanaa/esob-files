How to compare independent drinks brands

An editorial framework for price, availability, sourcing claims and everyday usefulness.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-10

Small brands often tell compelling stories, but a fair comparison still needs specific product information.

What to know

Check serving size, ingredients, stated origin and where a product can actually be purchased. Treat broad sustainability claims as questions requiring evidence.

Practical takeaway

When a product has not been independently sampled, we describe it as a product overview rather than a review.
