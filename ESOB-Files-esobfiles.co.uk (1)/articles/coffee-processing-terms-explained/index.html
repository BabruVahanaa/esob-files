Coffee processing terms explained

A concise introduction to washed, natural and honey processing without overpromising flavour.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-05

Processing describes steps taken after coffee cherries are harvested. Washed, natural and honey are broad categories, not exact flavour formulas.

What to know

Washed coffees are often discussed in terms of clarity, while natural processing can be associated with fruit-forward notes. Results vary widely with variety, growing conditions and execution.

Practical takeaway

Treat processing information as a clue to explore rather than a promise of specific tasting notes.
