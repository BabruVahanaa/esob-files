Reading sugar information on ready-to-drink beverages

How to compare labels across canned coffee, iced tea and soft drinks without relying on front-of-pack claims.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2025-12

Ready-to-drink beverages can vary substantially in sugar content even within the same category. A product described as natural or premium is not necessarily low in sugar.

What to know

Compare nutrition panels per 100 ml and per container. Serving sizes can obscure differences when one bottle contains more than one stated serving.

Practical takeaway

Caffeine content and ingredient lists also matter, especially when drinks combine coffee or tea extracts with other stimulants.
