How to plan a lower-waste weekly food shop

Simple ways to plan meals, store ingredients and reduce avoidable food waste without rigid rules.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 17 November 2026

Check what you already have

Before writing a shopping list, look through the fridge, freezer and cupboards. Note foods that need using soon and plan one or two meals around them. This prevents duplicates and makes the shopping list more realistic.

Plan flexible meals

A rigid seven-day menu can fail when schedules change. Instead, choose a few adaptable dishes that share ingredients: roasted vegetables can work in wraps, grain bowls or soups. Keep some longer-lasting staples for busy evenings.

Understand date labels

In the UK, a 'use by' date concerns food safety and should be followed; a 'best before' date is generally about quality. Storage instructions matter too. Do not rely on smell alone to decide whether food past a use-by date is safe.

Store food deliberately

Keep chilled foods at the temperature recommended by official food-safety guidance, and follow packaging directions after opening. Freeze suitable ingredients before they spoil, label containers with dates and rotate older items to the front.

Measure what changes

Track which foods you discard over a couple of weeks. If salad leaves repeatedly go unused, buy smaller amounts or choose a longer-lasting alternative. A lower-waste shop is not necessarily a zero-waste shop: practical habits beat perfection.
