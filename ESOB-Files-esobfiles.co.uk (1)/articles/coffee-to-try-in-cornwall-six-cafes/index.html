6. Little Hearts Café @ Courtyard, Saltash

Little Hearts Café @ Courtyard in Saltash is another place to explore for coffee. The café describes its offering as specialty coffee, with a focus on Brazilian beans grown at higher altitudes. Its stated profile is bold and distinctive; the exact flavour of a cup will depend on the beans, roast and preparation.

For location details, use Little Hearts Café on Google Maps, or visit the Little Hearts Café website. Ask the café directly about the current coffee selection and origins.
