Sparkling water and flavoured seltzers

What to look for beyond packaging and flavour names.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-07

Sparkling waters and flavoured seltzers can differ in mineral content, added sweeteners, acidity and flavouring.

What to know

Check ingredients and nutrition information when comparing products. A short ingredient list does not necessarily indicate better taste.

Practical takeaway

Serve chilled and compare products at the same temperature for a fairer tasting.
