Tea and food pairing for beginners

How sweetness, richness and bitterness influence simple pairings.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-10

Pairing tea with food is about balance rather than strict rules. Rich desserts may work with robust black tea, while lighter dishes can suit delicate styles.

What to know

Taste the tea before and after a bite of food. Notice whether the combination brings out sweetness, bitterness or astringency.

Practical takeaway

Personal preference is a more useful guide than a rigid chart.
