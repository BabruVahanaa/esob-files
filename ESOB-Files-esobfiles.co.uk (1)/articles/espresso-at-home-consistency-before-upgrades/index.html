Espresso at home: consistency before upgrades

How a simple workflow can improve results without buying more equipment.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-03

Espresso is sensitive to small changes in dose, grind and yield. A consistent routine is often more valuable than an immediate equipment upgrade.

What to know

Keep dose and beverage yield stable while changing grind size. Make sure the machine is properly heated and the basket is clean before interpreting taste.

Practical takeaway

Use the same coffee for several attempts before switching beans; this reduces variables.
