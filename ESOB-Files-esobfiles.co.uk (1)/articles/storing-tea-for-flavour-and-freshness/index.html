Storing tea for flavour and freshness

Simple storage habits for loose-leaf tea and everyday blends.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-04

Heat, moisture, strong odours and repeated exposure to air can damage tea quality over time.

What to know

Choose an opaque, well-sealed container in a cool, dry cupboard. Avoid storing tea next to strongly scented ingredients.

Practical takeaway

Buy quantities you can reasonably use rather than treating every tea as suitable for long-term ageing.
