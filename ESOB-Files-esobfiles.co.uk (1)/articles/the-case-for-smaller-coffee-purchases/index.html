The case for smaller coffee purchases

Why freshness, storage and actual consumption matter more than bulk discounts.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-08

Buying larger bags can reduce the price per gram, but savings are less compelling if coffee sits unused for months.

What to know

Estimate how many cups you make each week and choose a pack size accordingly. Store beans sealed, away from heat and moisture.

Practical takeaway

If you are exploring new coffees, smaller bags also make it easier to learn what you like.
