European café menus and changing drink choices

A perspective on how cafés balance classic drinks with seasonal and non-coffee options.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-06

Café menus increasingly need to serve customers with different preferences, including espresso drinkers, tea enthusiasts and people seeking caffeine-free choices.

What to know

A clear menu can explain ingredients, size and preparation without overwhelming guests with jargon. Seasonal drinks work best when the underlying flavour remains understandable.

Practical takeaway

This is an editorial observation, not a quantified claim about the European market.
