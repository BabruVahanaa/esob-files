Decaf coffee: methods and expectations

An accessible look at decaffeination and how to evaluate flavour without dismissing decaf.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-02

Decaf is not a single flavour category. Bean selection, processing, roasting and preparation still shape the result.

What to know

Some producers describe water-based or solvent-based decaffeination processes. These descriptions explain how caffeine is removed; they do not by themselves predict how the coffee will taste.

Practical takeaway

Treat decaf like any other coffee: check freshness, brew carefully and judge the result in the cup.
