Cold-brew tea at home

A simple method for making refreshing tea without excessive sweetness.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-05

Cold brewing uses cool water and time to extract flavour. It can be a convenient alternative to brewing hot tea and cooling it.

What to know

Use clean equipment, potable water and a covered container in the refrigerator. Begin with the tea maker’s guidance, then adjust strength to preference.

Practical takeaway

Keep prepared drinks refrigerated and follow sensible food-safety practices rather than leaving them at room temperature.
