Water temperature for green tea

Why cooler water can help and how to avoid rigid brewing rules.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-02

Many green teas benefit from water below a rolling boil, but there is no universal temperature that suits every leaf.

What to know

Start with the producer’s guidance where available. If the tea becomes overly bitter, reduce water temperature or steeping time before changing everything at once.

Practical takeaway

The shape and processing of the leaves can affect extraction as much as the number on a kettle display.
