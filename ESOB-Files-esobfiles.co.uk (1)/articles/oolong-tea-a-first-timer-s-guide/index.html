Oolong tea: a first-timer’s guide

How oxidation, roasting and brewing style create a wide range of oolong experiences.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-08

Oolong covers a broad family of teas with different levels of oxidation and roasting. Two oolongs can taste more different than two teas from separate headline categories.

What to know

Start with a modest leaf dose and follow the producer’s guidance. Some oolongs respond well to multiple short infusions.

Practical takeaway

Avoid judging the entire category from a single sample.
