Herbal infusions and true tea

Understanding the difference between Camellia sinensis tea and other botanical infusions.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Topic period: 2026-07

Black, green, white and oolong teas come from the tea plant, Camellia sinensis. Herbal infusions may use mint, rooibos, chamomile or other botanicals.

What to know

Caffeine content varies by ingredient and formulation. The term herbal does not guarantee that a blend contains no caffeine.

Practical takeaway

Read ingredient lists, particularly when managing allergies or sensitivities.
