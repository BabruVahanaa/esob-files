Coffee to try in Germany: styles and cafés to explore

A practical guide to coffee styles in Germany, with café names to research in Berlin and Hamburg.

By ESOB Files editorial desk · Published 10 November 2026 · Desk guide; check details before visiting

Travelling in Germany? Coffee culture varies from city to city and even between neighbouring cafés. In Berlin and Hamburg, you can explore drinks such as filter coffee, flat white, espresso. Availability is venue-specific.

What to order

Try a familiar espresso-based drink first, then compare it with a local favourite or a filter brew when offered. Ask about roast profile if you prefer chocolate-forward or fruit-forward cups.
