ABOUT & EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Useful information, not manufactured authority.

ESOB Files is a UK- and Europe-focused editorial project about coffee, tea and drinks.

Our editorial approach

We publish original guides, explainers and previews. We distinguish verified event details from unconfirmed arrangements and do not claim to have sampled products we have not tested. Historical topic periods describe the subject matter, not a false publication history.

Dates and archive

The initial 25-article collection was published on 10 November 2026. Each article includes a topic period spanning November 2025 to November 2026, and its real publication date. This is a retrospective editorial archive, not a record of previously published stories.

Commercial transparency

ESOB Files has a promotional relationship with Stronghearts and may link to stronghearts.co.uk where relevant. These links are included for reader context, not as independent third-party endorsements. If future content is sponsored or contains affiliate links, it should be labelled clearly.

Corrections and contact

We aim to correct errors promptly. An editorial contact address should be added before public launch so readers can request corrections. Do not treat this site as a source of verified product reviews unless an article explicitly documents an actual test.
